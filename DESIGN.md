# Aurelia House Design Context

## Scene and register

A boutique stay in historic George Town, experienced on a phone after a long flight or on a laptop while planning a quiet weekend. The surface is an editorial hospitality landing page whose single job is to make the booking feel calm and trustworthy.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#173a35` — primary dark surface and text.
- Ink two: `#102925` — footer depth.
- Cloud: `#f3f3ef` — neutral off-white content ground.
- Mist: `#dfe7df` — softened room and summary surface.
- Clay: `#c96243` — decisive booking accent.
- Sun: `#e5c78b` — warm, low-volume emphasis.

Display uses Playfair Display; UI and body use Manrope; labels use DM Mono. Runtime tokens live in `src/styles.css` and must be updated there with this document.

## Signature

The page uses the house as a narrative sequence: a deep-green arrival, pale daylight rooms, and a dark after-hours experience section. Typography carries the drama; buttons and cards stay disciplined.
